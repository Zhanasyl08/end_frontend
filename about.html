About Aura Shop

Aura Shop is a student project of an online store. The idea is simple: one website for clothes, gadgets and cosmetics.

We made this site for a web development course. The pages are connected with a menu, and the layout works on computer and phone.

What we sell

  • Clothes for every day
  • Small gadgets for study and home
  • Basic cosmetics

Why this shop

Many people buy these things in different places. We wanted one shop with a clear menu and simple pages.
